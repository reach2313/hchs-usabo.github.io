What We Do

Our core initiatives designed to drive high academic rigor and measurable community impact.

USABO Contest Preparation
Structured weekly workshops dissecting Campbell Biology, analytical problem-solving, and former USABO Open Exam questions to maximize student performance in national competitions.
Health Literacy Initiatives
Student-led community education programs focused on preventive medicine, cardiovascular health awareness, and basic biological literacy for middle school and local communities.
Resource Development & Web Platform
Creating open-source study guides, interactive diagnostic tools, and anatomical summary sheets accessible to all HCHS students preparing for advanced biology.
